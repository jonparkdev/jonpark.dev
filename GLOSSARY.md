# jonpark.dev

A public digital garden of whatever the author is learning. Courses such as Hello Interview organise parts of it; they don't define it. The garden follows the study; the study never follows the garden.

## Notes

**Note**:
One idea written up in the garden: the atom. It sits at exactly one Stage and belongs to at most one Topic. A Note with no Topic stands on its own, outside any Course.
_Avoid_: Post, article, page

**Stage**:
How well a Note is understood: Seedling, Budding, or Evergreen. It changes only through Recall events, never through polish.
_Avoid_: Status, level, maturity

**Seedling** (🌱):
The first Stage: "I'm thinking about this." Written from memory with the source closed.

**Budding** (🌿):
The second Stage: "I understand the core idea." Re-explained from memory on a different day and corrected against the source.

**Evergreen** (🌳):
The third Stage: "I can use this." Applied somewhere real, such as a Mock, a design problem, or an Explainer. Stable, not finished.

**Recall**:
Explaining an idea from memory with the source closed. The only thing that justifies moving a Note's Stage.
_Avoid_: Review, revision

**Promotion**:
Moving a Note up a Stage, earned by a Recall event.
_Avoid_: Using "promotion" for Upgrades

**Demotion**:
Moving a Note back a Stage, with the reason recorded. Expected, not a failure.

**Upgrade**:
A richer format added to a Note because of a felt gap: Diagram, then Interactive, then Explainer. Independent of Stage.
_Avoid_: Promotion

**Diagram**:
The first Upgrade: an Excalidraw sketch embedded in a Note.

**Interactive**:
An Upgrade that adds a hands-on piece to a Note.

**Explainer**:
An Upgrade that adds a video to a Note. Can count as the application event for Evergreen.

## Curriculum

**Course**:
An external body of study the author follows, such as Hello Interview. The garden can hold several. Each has exactly one Curriculum and organises only the Notes placed in it.
_Avoid_: Class, program

**Curriculum**:
A Course's syllabus as the garden arranges it: its Parts and Topics, plus the author's own Tangents.
_Avoid_: Syllabus (the Course's own outline, before Tangents)

**Part**:
An ordered group of Topics within a Curriculum, following its syllabus's grouping. Tangents form the last Part.

**Topic**:
One section of a Curriculum. A Note belongs to at most one Topic; having one is what places it in that Topic's Course.
_Avoid_: Section, chapter

**Topic index**:
A Course's page: every Topic in its Curriculum that has at least one Note, plus its Tangents, in Curriculum order, each listing its Notes with their Stages. Topics without Notes are not shown. It shows what has been learned, not progress through the syllabus.
_Avoid_: Progress map (the retired MVP page that marked every Topic, untouched ones included)

**Tended**:
A Note is tended when it was last changed. The home page lists every Note, most recently tended first.
_Avoid_: Recently tended (the retired five-Note list)

**Tangent** (🐇):
A Topic the author added to a Curriculum that isn't in its Course's syllabus, reached by following a rabbit hole from a Topic that is. Each Curriculum has its own Tangents. A rabbit hole with no parent Topic is just a Note with no Topic.
_Avoid_: Side quest, extra

## Practice

**Inbox**:
Private quick-capture list. Never published. Each line eventually becomes a Note or gets deleted.

**Gardening session**:
The weekly 45-minute session for clearing the Inbox, re-recalling Seedlings, and picking the next Mock.

**Mock**:
A timed attempt at a system design problem. A solo Mock is recorded and private; a human Mock is with another person.
